Plane Radar

    
The device fetches ADS-B positions itself over HTTPS, once per
      poll, and only while the radar is the screen on show. Nothing runs on your PC.


    

      
      
    

    
Your own position, in decimal degrees. In Google Maps, right-click
      where you are and the first entry on the menu is the pair, ready to paste.


    

      
Saved Locations

      

        
        
      

      

      
Keeps the position and range above under a name, stored on the
        device. Load applies them straight away. Up to 6.

    


    

      
      
    

    
    
    
Turn the dial to match how the device is sitting, and what is
      ahead of you in the room is ahead of you on screen. The N marker moves round
      the ring to wherever true north has ended up. 0 north, 90 east, 180 south,
      270 west.

    
One sweep of the screen takes exactly one poll, so the picture is
      repainted as the new positions arrive. Under 5 seconds the feed starts refusing.


    
    
ADS-B carries no route, so the destination is fetched by callsign -
      one extra request, and only the first time a flight is seen. The answer is kept
      while the aircraft stays around. Turn this off to keep the sweep running clean.
